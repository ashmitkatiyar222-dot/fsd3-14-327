Comprehensive Capabilities

Engineered for everyday peace of mind.

Ultra Wideband

See the distance. Follow the arrow.

Precision Finding uses Ultra Wideband technology to accurately measure the distance and direction between your iPhone and your AirTag. As you move, haptic feedback and visual cues guide you step by step.

Global Mesh Network

Hundreds of millions of helpers.

1 Billion+

Active Apple devices in the Find My network

When you leave something far behind, like at the beach or the gym, the Find My network helps track down your AirTag. Nearby Apple devices relay its location securely and anonymously to iCloud.

END-TO-END ENCRYPTED

The entire process is encrypted and anonymous. Not even Apple knows the location of your AirTag or the identity of the device that helps find it.

Splash, water, and dust resistant.

Got caught in a downpour? Spilled water on your backpack? AirTag is rated IP67 under IEC standard 60529 (maximum depth of 1 metre up to 30 minutes).

Long live the battery.

AirTag is designed to keep going for more than a full year on a standard, easily replaceable CR2032 coin cell battery. Your iPhone alerts you when it’s time for a swap.

Privacy

Built-in stalking prevention

Your iPhone detects if an unknown AirTag is traveling with you over time and sends an alert. If you still can’t locate it, the AirTag will start playing an audible sound.

No location history stored

Location data and history are never physically stored on the AirTag itself. All transmissions through the Find My network remain end-to-end encrypted.

Acoustics

Built-in speaker sound

Play a sound on the built-in speaker by going to the Items tab in the Find My app, or just say “Hey Siri, find my keys.”

NFC Lost Mode Tap

When someone finds your lost item, they can simply tap it using an NFC-capable smartphone to view your custom message and phone number.

Detailed Specs

Technical Specifications

Size and Weight
  • Diameter: 31.9 mm (1.26 inches)
  • Height: 8.00 mm (0.31 inch)
  • Weight: 11 grams (0.39 ounce)
Splash, Water, and Dust Resistance

Rated IP67 (maximum depth of 1 metre up to 30 minutes) under IEC standard 60529.

Connectivity
  • Bluetooth 5.0 for proximity finding
  • Apple-designed U1 chip for Ultra Wideband and Precision Finding
  • NFC tap for Lost Mode contact information
Speaker & Sensor
  • Built-in acoustic resonance speaker (85dB SPL)
  • Precision accelerometer sensor
Battery & Power

User-replaceable CR2032 coin cell battery. Lasts over one year based on everyday typical usage.

System Requirements & Compatibility
  • Apple ID account
  • iPhone and iPod touch models with iOS 14.5 or later
  • iPad models with iPadOS 14.5 or later
  • Precision Finding compatible with iPhone 11, 12, 13, 14, 15, and 16 series
Environmental Requirements

Operating ambient temperature: −20° to 60° C (−4° to 140° F)

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

AirTag sends out a secure Bluetooth signal that can be detected by nearby devices in the Find My network. These devices send the location of your AirTag to iCloud — then you can go to the Find My app and see it on a map. The whole process is anonymous and encrypted to protect your privacy.
AirTag is engineered to keep going for over a year on a standard CR2032 coin battery that you can easily replace yourself. Your iPhone lets you know when it’s time to pop in a new one.
While setting up and tracking an AirTag requires an Apple device with the Find My app, any NFC-equipped smartphone (including Android devices) can tap a lost AirTag to view the owner's contact info if Lost Mode is active.
AirTag is splash, water, and dust resistant, tested under controlled laboratory conditions with a rating of IP67 under IEC standard 60529 (maximum depth of 1 metre up to 30 minutes). Resistance is not permanent and may decrease as a result of normal wear.
AirTag is designed to track items, not people. To discourage unwanted tracking, your iOS or Android device will automatically notify you if an unknown AirTag is seen traveling with you over time.
