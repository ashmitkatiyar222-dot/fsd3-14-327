📜 HISTORIC MILESTONES & REFORMS

Lifelong Chronology of Service

An interactive journey through 76 years of unwavering devotion, grassroots organization, state governance in Gujarat, and leadership of Bharat into the 21st century.

🔍
Roots of Humility
17 September 1950

Birth in Vadnagar, Mehsana

Born to Damodardas Modi and Hiraba Modi in Vadnagar, Gujarat. Learned perseverance, discipline, and empathy for common citizens while helping at his father's tea stall near Vadnagar railway station.

🌱 Foundation of Service & Austerity
Organizational Strategy
1987 — 1991

Organizing Grassroots & Ekta Yatra

Appointed General Secretary of BJP Gujarat. Strategized key socio-cultural initiatives including the historic Ekta Yatra from Kanyakumari to Srinagar to unfurl the national flag at Lal Chowk.

🚩 Grassroots Mobilization
Governance Renaissance
7 October 2001

Sworn in as Chief Minister of Gujarat

Assumed charge following the catastrophic Bhuj earthquake. Orchestrated rapid post-disaster reconstruction, initiated 24x7 rural electrification (Jyotigram Yojana), and launched Vibrant Gujarat Global Summit.

⚡ 24x7 Power & Infrastructure Growth
National Transformation
May 2014

Historic Lok Sabha Mandate & Pradhan Sevak

Led BJP to an absolute majority. Sworn in as 14th Prime Minister. Launched PM Jan Dhan Yojana (over 50 Cr bank accounts opened), Swachh Bharat Abhiyan, and Make in India.

🏦 500 Million+ Bank Accounts Opened
Digital & Health Revolution
2016 — 2018

UPI Launch & Ayushman Bharat

Pioneered the Unified Payments Interface (UPI) creating the world's most advanced digital payment infrastructure. Rolled out Ayushman Bharat offering ₹5 Lakh free health cover per family to 55 Crore citizens.

🏥 550 Million Healthcare Beneficiaries
Global Leadership & Science
2019 — 2023

Chandrayaan-3 & Historic G20 Presidency

Administered over 220 Crore indigenous COVID-19 vaccine doses. Spearheaded Chandrayaan-3's historic lunar south pole landing (Shiv Shakti Point) and hosted the consensus-building G20 Summit in New Delhi.

🌕 First Nation at Moon's South Pole
Vision 2047
2024 — 2047

Architecting Viksit Bharat (Developed India)

Steering Bharat to become the world’s 3rd largest economy, a global semiconductor hub, bullet train operator, and a fully developed self-reliant nation by the centenary of independence in 2047.

🚀 Target 3rd Largest Global Economy
