🏆 Highest Civilian Honors Conferred

🇷🇺 2019

Order of St. Andrew the Apostle

Russia • Highest Civilian Award

Conferred for exceptional services in promoting a special and privileged strategic partnership between Russia and India.

🇫🇷 2023

Grand Cross of the Legion of Honour

France • Highest French Distinction

Presented by President Emmanuel Macron during PM Modi's historic Bastille Day visit in Paris.

🇧🇹 2024

Order of the Druk Gyalpo (Ngadag Pel gi Khorlo)

Bhutan • Highest Civilian Honor

First foreign head of government to receive Bhutan's highest honor for outstanding contribution to bilateral ties and regional growth.

🇦🇪 2019

Order of Zayed

United Arab Emirates • Highest Civil Decoration

Conferred for giving a momentous boost to strategic, economic, and cultural relations between the UAE and India.

🇪🇬 2023

Order of the Nile

Egypt • Highest State Honor

Bestowed by President Abdel Fattah el-Sisi in Cairo, recognizing PM Modi's contributions to global partnership and diplomacy.

🇺🇸 2020

Legion of Merit (Chief Commander)

United States • US Armed Forces Award

Awarded for exceptionally meritorious service and leadership in elevating the US-India strategic partnership and QUAD framework.
